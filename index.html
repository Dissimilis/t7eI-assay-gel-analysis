Method

Formula

% editing = 100 × (1 − √(1 − f)), with f = (b + c) / (a + b + c): a is the uncut parent band, b and c the two T7E1 cleavage products. This is the formula of Guschin et al. (2010, Methods Mol Biol 649:247–256) used in the NEB T7E1 protocol. It assumes random reannealing of a mix of different indels. For one dominant mutant allele the Single allele option uses f = 2p(1 − p) instead.

Measurement

Each lane is a vertical strip (default half the lane pitch). Its mean profile is smoothed, the parent band is the strongest peak, and the cleavage products are found at offsets shared by the lanes of the row, measured from each lane's own parent band, so tilted rows work. Areas are summed above a straight baseline between the valleys around each band, the way ImageJ Gel Analyzer is used by hand.

Checks

Clipped (saturated) parent bands, faint or missing products, weak lanes and bands cut by the region edge are flagged. A clipped parent makes % editing read too high. JPEG files from imagers are often contrast-stretched; a raw 16-bit TIFF gives more reliable numbers. Everything runs in this browser: images never leave the computer.
